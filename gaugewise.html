Accuracy vs precision

Imagine throwing darts. Accuracy means your darts land around the bullseye on average. Precision means your darts land close to each other. A good gauge needs both.

The red + marks the average of the darts.

In measurement terms: accuracy is called bias (how far off the average is), precision is called repeatability (how much readings wobble).
